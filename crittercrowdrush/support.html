Privacy Policy

Critter Crowd Rush is a crowd runner game: lead your crowd of cute animals through the right gates, win the clashes with rival animal gangs and beat the boss at the end of every level. If something is wrong, or you just want to tell us something about the game, write to the address below. It is read by the developer, not by a support desk, so please give it a day or two.

Contact: caesgames.dev@gmail.com

It helps to include your device model, your Android or iOS version and the level you were on. If the game closed by itself, tell us what you had just done.

Common questions

How do I play?

Drag your finger left and right to steer your crowd. In each row of gates, pick the gate that leaves you with the most critters. Gates with + or x make your crowd bigger, and gates with - or ÷ make it smaller. Sometimes both gates help, so pick the one that adds more; sometimes both hurt, so pick the one that takes away less. The first row of every level always has two good gates.

When you meet a rival animal gang, the bigger crowd wins the clash, but you lose as many critters as the gang had. If the numbers are equal, you lose. At the end of the road, a boss guards the finish line: you need more critters than the boss's number to beat it.

You can replay levels from the Levels map: tap the map button at the bottom of the home screen. The map shows your current level and up to 25 levels before it.

The game stopped when I left it

When you switch to another app, lock your phone or get a call, the run pauses so you don't lose it. Come back and, when you see Tap to continue, tap the screen to carry on.

I lost my progress, or it didn't move to my new phone

Your progress and settings are saved only on your device. The game has no account and no cloud save, so we can't see or restore your progress. If you uninstall the game or clear its data, your progress is gone, and it doesn't move to a new phone by itself. Your phone's own backup (Android's backup with Google, or iCloud Backup or a computer backup on iPhone) may include the game's data when you restore that backup, but that is controlled by your Google or Apple account, not by us.

Why do I see ads?

Ads keep the game free; it has no in-app purchases. After you complete level 3 and later levels, a full-screen ad may appear when you tap Next Level, at most once a minute. When you lose a level, you can choose to watch a video to skip it (the Skip Level button, which says "Watch an ad"); that is always your choice, and the level is skipped once the video has played to the end. If the Skip Level button doesn't appear, no video was available at that moment, so tap Try Again or check again a little later.

How do I turn off sound or vibration?

Use the sound and vibration buttons at the bottom of the home screen. The game remembers your choice.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and some US states), the game shows a Privacy Choices link at the bottom of the home screen. Tap it to reopen Google's privacy form and change your choice. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the same choice also turns the game statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase) on or off. On iPhone you can also turn tracking off at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. On Android you can delete or reset your advertising ID in your phone's Settings, under Privacy (or Google) and then Ads.

How do I delete my data?

Critter Crowd Rush by Cae's Games has no account and no server of its own. Your game data (level progress and settings) is stored only on your device.

Delete your game data yourself:

  1. Android: open Settings > Apps > Critter Crowd Rush > Storage and tap Clear data, or uninstall the game.
  2. iPhone: delete the app (touch and hold its icon, then Remove App > Delete App).

This removes all of the game's data from that device at once, and the next install gets a new ID for the game statistics. If your phone's own backup is turned on, a copy may stay in that backup and can come back when the game is reinstalled or the backup is restored.

Ask us to delete data we hold: email caesgames.dev@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data". We hold the emails you sent us, and game statistics in our Google Analytics account (privacy policy, section 5). The statistics are linked only to a random ID for an installation of the game, not to your name or email address, so we can't find them from your email; they are deleted automatically after 14 months. We delete your emails within 30 days of your request, unless the law requires us to keep them longer.

Data held by Google: the ads software and Google Analytics for Firebase send some data directly to Google (see the privacy policy). You can delete or reset your Android advertising ID in your phone's Settings, under Privacy (or Google) and then Ads, or turn off tracking on iPhone in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
